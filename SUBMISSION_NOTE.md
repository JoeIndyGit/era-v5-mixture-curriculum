# Submission Note

This repository distinguishes three evidence levels:

- **Completed:** the nine-run neural micro-proxy and internal accounting validation.
- **Published:** public dataset supplies used to defend feasibility.
- **Pre-registered:** the 1B and 3B transformer experiments, metrics and go/no-go gates.

No 1B or 3B result is invented. The mixture is a testable candidate and full-scale training remains gated by those experiments.
